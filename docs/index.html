Ostinato

The practice journal that stays out of your way.

Ostinato times your practice per piece, not just per session. Start from your iPhone, Apple Watch, Lock Screen or Siri, switch items with one tap, and see where your time goes week by week.

  • Time per piece and exercise, with tempo progress
  • Apple Watch app that works without your iPhone nearby
  • Live Activity, widgets, Control Center and Siri
  • Metronome, tuner and drone built in; record yourself
  • Free. No account, no ads, no tracking

Privacy policy · Source code · Support

Ostinato doesn't collect any data. Your practice stays on your devices and your own iCloud.
