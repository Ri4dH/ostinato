Ostinato Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Ostinato doesn't collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code that sends information anywhere.

What stays on your devices

Your practice sessions, repertoire, notes, instruments, goals and recordings are stored on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud and leave "Sync with iCloud" on, your data syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud account (CloudKit and iCloud Drive). Apple stores this data under your Apple Account; the developer can't see or access it. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings → iCloud. Data already on your device is kept.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you record yourself or use the tuner. Tuner audio is analyzed live and never saved. Recordings are saved only on your devices or in your own iCloud Drive, and you can delete them at any time.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your devices unless you choose to share it, for example by exporting a CSV or PDF report and sending it to a teacher.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your Data → Delete All Data removes everything from the app and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Children

Ostinato is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/Ri4dH/ostinato/issues.
