## Description

Ostinato is a practice journal for musicians that stays out of your way.

Start a session, then tap the piece or exercise you're working on. Ostinato times each one separately, so you can see that today was 10 minutes of scales, 20 on the étude and 30 on the Chaconne, and how that adds up over weeks.

TIME PER PIECE
• Switch items with one tap while the timer runs
• Per-item totals, weekly minutes and tempo history
• Edit any segment afterwards: rename, reassign, adjust minutes

A TIMER YOU CAN TRUST
• Keeps exact time when you switch apps, lock your phone, take a call, or the app is closed
• Pick up where you left off after a restart

APPLE WATCH
• Start, switch items, pause and finish from your wrist
• Works without your iPhone nearby and syncs when it's back
• Optional taps every few minutes as a reminder to switch

BUILT INTO YOUR DEVICE
• Live Activity and Dynamic Island with pause and next item
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center button
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Log 20 minutes of scales in Ostinato"

TOOLS
• Sample-accurate metronome with tap tempo, any time signature and subdivisions
• Chromatic tuner with adjustable A4 (415–466 Hz), transposing instruments and custom tunings
• Drone in all 12 keys with fifths and octaves, playing alongside the metronome
• Record yourself; Ostinato estimates tempo and key on your device

STATS
• Minutes per day, per instrument, per item and per category
• Streaks with rest days built in, so a day off doesn't undo your week
• Calendar of practice days, rating and focus trends

PRIVATE AND FREE
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no subscription
• Your data stays on your devices and syncs only through your own iCloud
• Export CSV for your teacher, a PDF weekly report, or a full backup
